Interlocked letterforms, exaggerated proportions, and deliberate negative space. Three expressive directions for cybrdelic, built around recognition as a brand mark. Readability is secondary.

RECOMMENDED

01 / Fluid sigil

Heavy flowing forms pinch into sharp tips. A deep hooked descender and interlocked middle give the name an asymmetric silhouette.

02 / Cut blackletter

Angular joints, tall archways, and deep cutouts. The most aggressive and architectural direction.

03 / Optical mass

A wide, compressed silhouette with stretched bowls and narrow slits. The most abstract and psychedelic direction.
