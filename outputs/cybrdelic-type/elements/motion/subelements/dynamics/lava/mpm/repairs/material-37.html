What changed, and what remains

The new basalt surface contains actual cavities and relief instead of a smooth polygon shell. Emission is calculated from temperature and sampled from the geometry. A separate test compares the emitter with Mitsuba’s built-in area light.

The rock temperature profile uses a one-dimensional enthalpy solve with radiation, convection and latent heat. Its preparation age is an authored initial condition. This thermal revision is not fed back into the cached mechanical motion; coupled rock–melt heat exchange is still missing.

The separate continuous-crust run reached 2.2 seconds with no open fractures, then failed its pressure-solve acceptance check. It is not presented as a successful lava result. Broad slab geometry and sustained, physically generated breakup remain the main unresolved defects.
