# Motion-study review

Baseline: the existing element videos reveal preformed letter geometry (water/earth) or continuously source a letter mask (air). Uniform staging and release made the material seem arranged.

Iteration 1: actual FLIP moving source at time scale 0.18 with inherited velocity 0.08 produced excessive sheet expansion and cropped splashes. Rejected; bake stopped after visual inspection of frame 48.

Iteration 2: inherited velocity removed, source radius reduced from 0.135 to 0.095, tangent velocity reduced from 0.45 to 0.12, time scale 0.12, source subframes increased to 8, lens widened from 42 to 36 mm. No letter mesh, mask reveal, or coordinated fragment collapse. Source trajectory remains artist-authored from approved mark 01.

Early visual result: contained stream and independent deformation. Remaining limitation: narrow liquid sections look tubular; the setting still resembles a studio. This is a six-second 1080p motion study, not a finished replacement for all six intros or a claim of photorealism. Real-time camera and emitter movement use a slow-time fluid simulation, rather than a literal high-speed camera capture of a single time scale.

Original six exports preserved. Cache integrity and final video metadata recorded separately.
