## Water

The earlier water source placed parcels directly in the finished geometry with very little velocity. Three CPU trials of a translated travelling guide were rejected: large travel spoiled the held silhouette, while the restrained version still resembled a reveal. The retained inlet is backtraced along the curved stroke direction and injects more tangential momentum. Edge parcels arrive slightly after the central flow.

During the hold, two smooth curl-force fields drive resolved circulation. The bulk drag eases after formation, while the normal containment remains stronger. These are authored bending forces applied before the native APIC/FLIP pressure projection. They are not teleportation or a rendered mesh morph. The underlying water does not naturally spell typography without those controls. Capillary optical normal detail remains the same subgrid approximation as r3.

The native surface reconstruction omits under-resolved isolated markers instead of showing an artificial sphere cloud. Native accounting still includes those parcels. At 5.8 seconds the support releases and gravity takes over.

## Lightning

This is an authored discharge visualization, not calibrated plasma or atmospheric lightning physics.
