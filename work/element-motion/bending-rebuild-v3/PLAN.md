# Focused water and lightning revision

Visual defects: uniform bead-like spray; excessive sheet fragmentation; no camera exposure motion blur; lightning inherits an artificial curved-strip topology and has no participating atmosphere.

Scope: new v3 solver, reconstruction, rendering, and the two corresponding gallery sources. Keep earth, fire, air, and previous videos intact. Maximum four look iterations per element; inspect matched pilot frames before complete rendering.

Capture: Python water mesh preparation; Blender frame 42 and 75 pilots; CPU lightning frames 18, 24, 37–39, 44, 50. Inspect resized images and temporal contact sheets. Verify finite states, solver residuals, mass representation, frame counts, video metadata, and browser playback.

Water: higher spatial resolution; lower injected transverse strain; thicker resolved sheet; stable droplet classification across frames; actual velocity-driven shutter integration. A change must reduce the visible bead/snow appearance, not just increase particle count.

Lightning: grow discharges in the actual world-space conductivity corridor; separate stepped leaders/return strokes; 3D advected low-density aerosol with pressure-projected velocity and channel lighting. Haze is an authored aerosol, not smoke generated by clean electrical discharge. Do not obscure the channel or raise the black background.

Disk: retain simulation states and final media; stream reconstruct/render intermediates and remove only exact generated temporary mesh files after successful frame render. Never remove previous deliverables.
