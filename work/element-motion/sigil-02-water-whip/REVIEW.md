Scope: the opening movement of water. Preserve the actual 02 target, clear-water
material, black backdrop, dark floor, hold and gravity release. Earth, fire, air
and lightning are out of scope.

Baseline defect: r5 raises a uniformly thick tube into one symmetric arch, then
blends it into the target. The motion has no distinct leading impulse or turn.

Candidate: begin with a rounded low crescent. Hook the leading end up and back,
bank toward the camera, cast across frame and let the tail follow through.
Arc-length and volume-aware cross sections supply directed force goals. Native
FLIP transports the complete initial mass; there is no timed emitter or vertex
reveal. The guide is authored, not an emergent simulation of an unseen bender.

Budget: up to three CPU preview revisions before a single full water render.
Gate: inspect CPU motion frames, verify finite pressure-converged closed mass,
floor containment, coherent surface, a changing asymmetric silhouette, and a
continuous arrival into 02. Full render must also pass decode, visual review and
