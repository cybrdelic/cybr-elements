# Grounded bending / 02

User defect: the prior formation was a reveal. Water and earth also needed visible floors and impacts.

Scope: water and earth only. Preserve the actual 02 artwork, the accepted fire/air movies, lightning, and the r4 comparisons.

Water starts with its entire mass in one visible ribbon on the floor. Moving material guides exert bounded external acceleration through the native APIC/FLIP solver. They lift and bend that ribbon into the artwork, then blend into a static confinement field with circulation. At release the bending field goes to zero. Gravity, pressure, free surface, solid-floor contact, and a dissipative unresolved wall layer govern the fall and puddle. There is no timed glyph-wide emitter, particle-position assignment, outflow deletion, or render morph. The supernatural bending force is authored, not an emergent fluid effect. The wall layer is an approximation, not a resolved viscous boundary layer.

Earth starts with all major fractures visible on the floor. Its powered lift/roll and suspended hold are authored rigid trajectories. Native Bullet handles released inter-body collisions, floor collisions, bouncing, sliding, and settling. Small ground gravel is dynamic for the whole shot. The guided approach is kinematic, not a force-driven granular solve. Before release, the suspended stress wave settles back into the fitted fracture poses; all fragments then become dynamic together. The CPU handoff check recorded no upward release kick (maximum rise below 0.000001 world units before/through early contact).

Surface reconstruction renders resolved fluid surfaces. Isolated markers below reconstruction resolution are retained by the simulation but are not turned into an artificial bead cloud. The video audit records that unresolved fraction; fixed particle count alone is not a proof of rendered volume conservation.

Both use the existing reviewed Cycles materials on a black surrounding world. Floor geometry matches the collision height. Endings retain the resulting puddle or rubble rather than fading or deleting it.

Validation: reduced CPU simulation/reconstruction and Cycles frames before production; inspect formation, hold, impact and rest; require finite converged water projection, fixed particle count, no floor penetration, finite Bullet poses, complete video decode, correct 1080p/30fps metadata. Visual review is separate from numeric gates. Maximum three CPU contact/formation variants before choosing the final candidate. User acceptance remains pending.
